01THE SAGA

Three games. One man. A trail of bodies through the smoking ruins of his own life.

New York detective Max Payne loses his wife and baby daughter to a mob conspiracy's Valkyr experiment — then spends years drowning the grief in bitter justice and cheap painkillers.

From the frozen streets of Manhattan to the favelas of São Paulo, Max carves through conspiracies like a bullet through fog — leaving only dead bodies and monologues that read like hard-boiled poetry.
